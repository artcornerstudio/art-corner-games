## 2. Pricing rules (rules are LIVE; numbers are Jean's)

1. **Single source:** one approved pricing matrix lives in this document (§3) once Jean supplies it. No agent quotes from memory, comparables, or estimates.
2. **No invention:** if a required rate is missing, the proposal stops at PREPARE with `[AWAITING JEAN: rate]` and appears in the Founder Control Center. Nothing with a placeholder ever reaches a customer.
3. **Discount authority:** any deviation from the approved matrix — discounts, bundles, comps, "friend prices" — is a **Jean approval gate**, no exceptions and no thresholds until Jean sets them.
4. **Scope changes reprice:** any change to scope after quoting reopens the quote; the Proposals & Quotes agent re-prepares, Finance reviews, Jean approves.
5. **Margin & capacity check:** Finance, Pricing & Capacity Review signs every quote before it is queued for Jean, checking (a) matrix conformity, (b) capacity fit against Jean's calendar/workload limits, (c) cost inputs (materials, travel, lift rental, etc. — actuals or Jean-supplied, never guessed).
6. **Deposits & terms:** payment terms and deposit percentages are part of the matrix; contract terms are always a Jean gate.
7. **Public projects:** municipal/RFP pricing is always custom and always Jean-approved line by line.
8. **Youth programs:** school/youth program pricing must also pass Safety, Rights & Youth-Safety Review (no terms that imply collecting minor data for pricing purposes).

**Mural terms (approved by Jean, 2026-10-17)**
- **Design fee** is billed separately from painting and is owed even if the client does not proceed to painting.
- **Revisions:** 2 rounds of minor revisions included in the design fee; further rounds or major conceptual changes billed at the extra-revision hourly rate.
- **Materials:** standard paint and sundries included in the per-sq-ft rate. Specialty items (metallic paint, gold leaf, anti-graffiti clear coat) billed as separate line items at cost.
- **Equipment:** scaffolding and lifts billed to the client at cost.
- **Wall prep** (cleaning, patching, scraping, priming) billed extra; the client may instead prep the wall to Jean's written specifications.
- **Travel:** free within the travel radius; beyond it, round-trip mileage at the **current-year IRS rate** (look up at quote time; never assume). Lodging billed if the site is more than 90 minutes' drive each way.
- **Payment:** projects up to $3,000 — 50% before work begins, 50% on completion. Projects over $3,000 — 33% non-refundable deposit to book and start design, 33% on the first on-site painting day, 34% on completion and final walkthrough.

## 4. Capacity rules

### Jean's bookable hours (set by Jean, 2026-10-17)

| Days | Window | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Monday–Friday | 5:30 pm – 7:00 pm | 1.5 hr × 5 = 7.5 hr |
| Saturday–Sunday | 11:00 am – 12:30 pm | 1.5 hr × 2 = 3 hr |
| **Weekly total available** | | **10.5 hr** |

- **Weekly booking cap:** 12 hours (Jean). Because the windows total 10.5 hours, the windows are the binding limit.
- **Never book outside these windows** (Jean: "don't book off hours"). No agent may propose, hold, or confirm any time outside them. Work that cannot fit (e.g., multi-day mural painting, events, on-site senior-home sessions at the venue's preferred time) is routed to Jean as a scheduling exception in the digest — never squeezed in or pre-offered.
- Each window fits exactly one 1 hr 30 min lesson → **7 lesson slots per week** maximum.


- Jean's production hours are the scarce resource. Finance & Capacity maintains a simple weekly capacity ledger (hours committed vs. the windows above; agents never assume availability beyond them).
- No agent may commit delivery dates. Dates are proposed from the capacity ledger and confirmed only by Jean or a Jean-approved policy.
- Over-capacity qualified leads are not declined by agents — they are queued with a recommendation (waitlist / refer / decline) for Jean.
