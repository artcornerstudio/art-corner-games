## 2. Pricing rules (rules are LIVE; numbers are Jean's)

1. **Single source:** one approved pricing matrix lives in this document (§3) once Jean supplies it. No agent quotes from memory, comparables, or estimates.
2. **No invention:** if a required rate is missing, the proposal stops at PREPARE with `[AWAITING JEAN: rate]` and appears in the Founder Control Center. Nothing with a placeholder ever reaches a customer.
3. **Discount authority:** any deviation from the approved matrix — discounts, bundles, comps, "friend prices" — is a **Jean approval gate**, no exceptions and no thresholds until Jean sets them.
4. **Scope changes reprice:** any change to scope after quoting reopens the quote; the Proposals & Quotes agent re-prepares, Finance reviews, Jean approves.
5. **Margin & capacity check:** Finance, Pricing & Capacity Review signs every quote before it is queued for Jean, checking (a) matrix conformity, (b) capacity fit against Jean's calendar/workload limits, (c) cost inputs (materials, travel, lift rental, etc. — actuals or Jean-supplied, never guessed).
6. **Deposits & terms:** payment terms and deposit percentages are part of the matrix; contract terms are always a Jean gate.
7. **Public projects:** municipal/RFP pricing is always custom and always Jean-approved line by line.
8. **Youth programs:** school/youth program pricing must also pass Safety, Rights & Youth-Safety Review (no terms that imply collecting minor data for pricing purposes).

## 4. Capacity rules

- Jean's production hours are the scarce resource. Finance & Capacity maintains a simple weekly capacity ledger (hours committed vs. available, set by Jean; default assumption: **unknown until Jean states it** — agents must not assume availability).
- No agent may commit delivery dates. Dates are proposed from the capacity ledger and confirmed only by Jean or a Jean-approved policy.
- Over-capacity qualified leads are not declined by agents — they are queued with a recommendation (waitlist / refer / decline) for Jean.
