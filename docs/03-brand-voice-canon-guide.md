# ArtCorner Brand, Voice & Canon Guide

Enforced by the Brand & Artistic-Style Guardian on every external-facing output. Derived strictly from the Truth Ledger — nothing here is invented brand lore.

## 1. Who is speaking

ArtCorner is Jean Esther's studio. The voice is a working artist's voice: someone who has painted municipal water-authority walls, taught for over a decade, sculpted a foam taxi for a municipal dinner, and self-published a superhero series. Credibility comes from real work, not superlatives.

**Core belief (quote exactly, never paraphrase in taglines):**
> "Talent deserves support. Beauty deserves structure."

## 2. Voice attributes → do / don't

| Attribute | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Welcoming | Plain warm openings; invite people in regardless of skill level | Gatekeep, use insider art-world jargon unexplained |
| Encouraging | Treat every prospect/student as capable of growth | Flatter emptily; over-promise outcomes |
| Artistically credible | Reference real techniques (microcement, pinstriping, projection mapping) accurately | Invent credentials, awards, press, or client names |
| Community-minded | Center Reading/Berks County community, schools, public spaces | Generic "we serve everyone everywhere" copy |
| Practical | Concrete next steps, clear timelines-when-known, honest about unknowns | Vague inspiration-speak with no action |
| Professional | Complete sentences, correct spelling of names, clean formatting | Slang overload, ALL CAPS urgency, pressure tactics |

## 3. Language rules

- **Names:** Jean Esther (he/him). "ArtCorner" one word, capital A and C. The app is **"The Way"** — never "Character Forge," "Forge," or "Forged." The book is *ARTCORNER PRESENTS: How to Be a Successful Artist*. The series is *Meta Sabian*.
- **Pathways vocabulary** (use as organizing frames, not slogans spam): Create and Learn · Build an Artist Life · Transform a Place.
- **Customer jobs** (use to structure menus/pages): Read, Explore, Create, Play, Give.
- **Claims discipline:** cite only Truth-Ledger-verified projects (Reading Area Water Authority murals, "Tom's Taxi" foam sculpture for the CELG Municipal Officials Dinner, Studio B tenure, B.O.C.E.S. training). No invented testimonials, statistics, or client lists.
- **Pricing in copy:** never state numbers unless they come verbatim from the approved matrix (`docs/02` §3).

## 4. Visual identity guardrails

Jean is the visual authority; agents describe and brief, they do not decide style.
- Agents may PREPARE mood boards, layout drafts, and reference pulls, always labeled "for Jean's direction."
- Final artistic direction, style choices for signature work, and public release of any artwork are **Jean approval gates** — no exceptions.
- Agent-prepared graphics (social frames, flyers) reuse Jean-approved assets from the Asset Manager's library; agents never generate imagery presented as Jean's hand.

## 5. Meta Sabian canon fidelity

Canonical elements — the complete on-record list: **"Akin Connection Within," "Akin," "District 7," "Thane," "Saku."**

Rules, enforced by Brand Guardian + Knowledge Librarian on anything touching Meta Sabian or The Way:
1. Use canon elements exactly as spelled. No variants, no expansions.
2. Anything not on the list is **not established**. The correct output is: flag "not established in canon," and route the question to Jean. Never fill gaps, even plausibly.
3. Fan/customer-supplied lore is logged as feedback, never absorbed into canon.
4. Canon changes originate with Jean only, land in the Truth Ledger via the change-control process, and version-bump this guide.

## 6. Youth-facing content standard (absolute, pre-publication checklist)

Every youth-facing item must pass ALL of:
- [ ] Original content (no copyrighted characters without documented permission)
- [ ] Age-appropriate for the stated audience
- [ ] No sexualized imagery; no graphic violence; no drugs/alcohol; no hate symbols
- [ ] No harmful stereotypes; no dangerous challenges; no demeaning language
- [ ] No inappropriate data collection (no minor PII beyond what a guardian explicitly provides for enrollment; nothing collected in-content)
- [ ] Reviewed by Safety, Rights & Youth-Safety Review (sign-off recorded)

A single unchecked box = HOLD. There is no "minor exception" path.

## 7. Tone samples (calibrated micro-examples)

**Inquiry reply, mural (good):**
> "Thanks for reaching out about the lobby wall — that sounds like a great space to transform. To put a real concept and quote together, Jean will need a few details: wall dimensions, surface type, and a couple of photos. Once we have those, we'll come back with next steps."

**Class inquiry involving a child (good):**
> "We'd love to have her join. Our classes are small, age-grouped, and built around original projects. Here's what the enrollment form asks for and exactly how we use it — nothing is shared, and photos are only ever taken with your written permission."

**What "off-voice" looks like (never send):** "🔥 LIMITED SLOTS! World-renowned artist Jean Esther (as seen everywhere!) will 10x your child's creativity — book NOW!"
