# Founder Control Center — Specification

Purpose: Jean's entire routine involvement in operations compressed into **one daily digest, ≤15 minutes**. Compiled by the Orchestrator. Mockup: `founder-control-center.html` (repo) — published as a private artifact page.

## Delivery modes (tool-honest)
- **LIVE today:** a markdown/HTML digest file compiled during any working session, and an artifact page Jean opens on his phone.
- **READY FOR APPROVAL:** a scheduled daily session (Routine) that compiles it automatically each morning.
- **NEEDS CONNECTION:** email delivery of the digest.

## Digest sections (fixed order — Jean learns one shape)

1. **Decisions awaiting approval** (max 5, ranked by revenue/deadline impact). Each: one-line ask, context in ≤3 lines, agent recommendation, APPROVE / EDIT / DECLINE / DISCUSS.
2. **Projects at risk** — anything red/amber on deadline, capacity, or customer sentiment, with the single unblocking action.
3. **Qualified opportunities** — new leads that passed qualification, with fit score and recommended response.
4. **Upcoming commitments** — next 7 days: classes, events, deadlines, site visits.
5. **Customer issues** — open issues, age, owner, state.
6. **Revenue & capacity indicators** — recorded bookings, pipeline at PREPARE, Jean-hours committed next 2 weeks vs. stated availability. ("No baseline yet" shown honestly while observing.)
7. **Content awaiting publication** — certified items in the PREPARE queue (post/email/listing/page) — one tap to approve each.
8. **Agent & integration health** — failures, stale items, connector outages. Silence is reported as silence ("0 exceptions"), never hidden.
9. **Recommended actions** — Orchestrator's ≤3 suggestions that are *not* approvals (e.g., "set Tuesday availability", "supply the missing rate for S3").

## Rules
- **≤5 decisions per digest.** Overflow waits, ranked; P0 safety items are the only interrupt that bypasses the digest.
- Every decision line names its approval gate from the Authority Matrix.
- Jean's responses are logged verbatim by the Orchestrator; EDIT responses go to the Librarian's feedback log (never silent ledger rewrites).
- A digest is compiled even on a quiet day ("nothing awaiting you") so absence of the digest itself signals a system failure.

## 15-minute budget
Sections 1 (7 min) · 2–5 (4 min) · 6–8 (2 min) · 9 (2 min). If a digest can't be read in 15 minutes, the Orchestrator is over-including — that's a defect QA tracks.
