# Measurement Scorecard

Owner: Analytics & Performance (06). Principle: **baselines come from observation, never fabrication.** Every metric below launches in state "no baseline yet — observing (day N)" and earns a baseline only after its observation window.

## Headline metric

**Founder hours: required vs. saved.**
- *Required:* minutes of Jean-touch per item (digest reading, approvals, edits, escalations, direct work the system requested of him) — logged by the Orchestrator per digest response and per gated decision.
- *Saved:* observed counterfactual — for each task class, Jean records once (during onboarding week) how long it used to take him manually (e.g., "answering a mural inquiry with questions: ~20 min"). Saved = (his stated manual time − his actual touch time) × volume. Stated times are labeled "Jean-reported baseline," never industry guesses.
- Reported weekly: total required, total saved, net, and trend.

## Full scorecard

| Metric | Definition (source) | Cadence | Baseline method |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conversion | Qualified leads → accepted proposals (intake + Jean decisions log) | Monthly | 60-day observation |
| Revenue | Recorded bookings only (Finance records) | Monthly | Accumulates from launch; no history invented |
| Margin | Per project: price − recorded costs (only once matrix + cost records exist) | Monthly | Blocked until matrix populated — shown as "blocked" honestly |
| Capacity utilization | Jean's committed hours ÷ his stated available hours | Weekly | Blocked until Jean states availability |
| Turnaround time | Per workflow: intake → first substantive response; intake → proposal; approval → delivery (workflow timestamps) | Weekly | 30-day observation |
| CSAT | Post-delivery feedback responses (approved form, verbatim) | Per delivery | 10 responses minimum before an average is shown |
| Revision rate | QA rejections per certified deliverable; client proof rounds vs. scoped rounds | Monthly | 30-day observation |
| Safety incidents | Count + severity from Safety Review reports; **target is 0 and every incident gets a Risk Register entry** | Continuous | Absolute count from day 1 |
| Digest health | Digest read time (Jean-reported), decisions per digest, overflow queue depth | Weekly | 2-week observation |
| Agent health | HOLD frequency, stale items, rejection rates per agent | Weekly | 30-day observation |

## Reporting rules
- Numbers without sources don't ship; projections are labeled projections and never mixed into actuals.
- Missing data is reported as missing ("no CSAT responses yet"), not smoothed.
- The weekly note names one bottleneck and one recommendation — metrics exist to change decisions, and any metric that hasn't changed a decision in a quarter is proposed for retirement.
