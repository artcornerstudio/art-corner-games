# Mural Pricing — Market Benchmarks (reference only)

Supplied by Jean, 2026-10-17, from his own research (sources cited: Sketchar, Book An Artist, Scribd).
**Status: REFERENCE — NOT APPROVED PRICING.** These are industry ranges, not ArtCorner rates. Quotes may only use the numbers Jean records in `docs/02-service-catalog-pricing-rules.md` §3.

| Topic | Benchmark range / norm |
|---|---|
| Method | Per square foot (most standard and transparent) |
| Rural rate, standard–moderate complexity | $10–$25 / sq ft (national $10–$50+) |
| Simple color-blocking | $10–$15 / sq ft |
| Intricate illustration or lettering | $20–$25 / sq ft |
| Photorealism / detailed portraiture | $35–$50+ / sq ft |
| Exterior premium | +$2–$5 / sq ft (weather, exterior paints, UV/weather sealant) |
| Minimum project fee | $500–$1,000 |
| Design fee | Separate, flat $150–$500 by complexity |
| Revisions | 1–2 rounds of minor revisions included; more, or major changes, at $50–$75 / hr |
| Materials | Standard paint and sundries included in the rate; specialty items (metallics, gold leaf, anti-graffiti coat) billed separately |
| Equipment | Scaffolding / lifts billed to client at cost |
| Wall prep | Billed extra: hourly, or flat $100–$1,000 by condition; client may prep to spec |
| Travel | Included within 20–30 miles; beyond, IRS mileage rate round trip (research cited $0.67/mi — verify the current-year IRS rate); lodging billed if over 90 min each way |
| Payment | 50% upfront / 50% on completion; projects over $3,000: 33% non-refundable deposit, 33% on first on-site day, 34% on completion |
