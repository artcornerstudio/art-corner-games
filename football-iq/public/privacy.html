Privacy and refunds

Plain-language version. Last updated October 2026.

The short version

Football IQ collects nothing about your child. There is no sign-up, no account, no ads, and nothing a kid does in the game is sent anywhere. Everything the game remembers (lessons passed, scores, badges, settings, plays you design) is saved only in your own browser, on your own device. The one exception is a grown-up buying the full game: then we keep a small purchase record, described below.

What we collect

Nothing about players. Our server does not record who plays, how often, or how well. We use no analytics, no ad networks, and no tracking pixels. The only thing we keep is the purchase record under "Purchases and refunds", and only when a grown-up buys the full game.

What is saved on your device

The game uses your browser's built-in storage to remember, only on the device you are playing on:

  • Lessons passed, quiz scores, badges, and best game scores
  • Your difficulty, sound, and read-aloud settings
  • Plays you design, and your season record
  • An offline copy of the game and of the voice clips you have already heard

None of this leaves your device. Clearing your browser's site data, or using a private window, erases it. The Coach view for grown-ups can export your progress as a file you keep yourself.

Accounts, ads, and other companies

There are no accounts and no passwords. No ads, ever. While you play, the game does not call any outside service: no analytics, no font downloads, no images from other websites. "Ask Coach" answers are written on your device from the play's own facts.

"Read aloud" plays Coach Eric's recorded clips from this site, or your device's built-in voice. Some browsers run their built-in voices through the browser maker's own service. The only text spoken is the lesson, question, or result on screen, never anything about you.

Purchases and refunds

The free demo never asks for anything. The full game is a one-time purchase of $9.99 (USD). There is no subscription and nothing renews. It unlocks all 9 units and 39 lessons, all 8 mini games, tackle and flag football, the Coach view with printable play cards, and Coach Eric's voice for every lesson. It works on every device where you enter your license code.

The payment happens on Stripe's secure checkout page, not in the game. Stripe handles the card details and sends the receipt; we never see card numbers. Our server keeps one small record of the purchase (the email address used at checkout, the amount, and the date) so we can unlock the game and honor refunds. It also sets one sign-in cookie on the device that unlocked the game. When you buy, the game shows you a license code once. Keep it: it unlocks the game on another device. Nothing a player does in the game is ever sent to us.

30-day money-back guarantee. If you are not happy for any reason, email Artcorner@me.com within 30 days of buying, from or mentioning the email address you used at checkout, and we will refund you in full. No questions asked. Once a refund is issued, the full game locks again on every device.

Kids and COPPA

Football IQ is for kids roughly ages 8 to 16, and for parents and coaches. We collect no personal information from children: nothing a child types or does in the game leaves the device. The only personal information we receive is a grown-up's email address at checkout, used only to confirm the purchase and handle refunds. We never sell or share it. Purchases are for grown-ups: the game asks a grown-up question before it opens checkout, and kids should not buy anything without a parent or guardian.

Changes to this page

If this policy ever changes, we will update the date at the top. Because the game collects nothing from kids today, any change that added data collection would be a big one, and we would make it very clear.

Questions?

Parents and coaches can reach us any time: Artcorner@me.com
