🧑‍🏫 How to play (for grown-ups too)

Get Open! — Drag your gold player (or use arrow keys). Stay away from the red defenders, keep out of their way between you and the ball, and don't hug the ball-carrier — spread out! When your ring turns green, hold your spot and the pass comes to you.

Pick the Pass! — Look before you kick! Tap the teammate who is open. If a defender is in the passing lane or guarding them, they'll steal it — and the game shows you why, so you learn for next time. Get passes right 3 in a row to earn a star! ⭐

Where Do I Stand? — The field lights up into three zones: defense 🛡️ (protect our goal), midfield 🔗 (the middle), and striker ⚡ (up by their goal). Run to your position — and when the ball moves to one side, slide over with it, just like a real team!

Watch the defenders: dark-red chasers (!) hunt YOU — sprint away from them. Orange blockers (X) stand in the passing lane — slide sideways to open a new lane.
