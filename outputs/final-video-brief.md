### The 90 minutes

| Minutes | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–8 | **The Leak Number.** Everyone posts monthly inquiry count and average job value — **anonymous poll, not chat** (see F18). Anchors the session. |
| 8–22 | **Gate 1 — Intake.** Your scope questions: size, surface, deadline, usage rights, revision count. **Checkpoint: everyone posts their live form URL.** |

**Three public checkpoints, not twenty private screens.** The room self-evidences: at each checkpoint you either have the artifact or you say so in chat and get unblocked by exception. That mechanism holds at scale, and it means nobody quietly falls behind and discovers it at minute 88.

You do not get a seat by paying. You get a seat by clearing three things, and the checkout says so **before** you buy:

1. **A live way to take payment online** — Stripe, PayPal or Square. Fifteen minutes to set up. *We cannot run your test transaction without it and I will not pretend otherwise.*
2. **Three real prices decided.** Base rate, rush fee, revision cap. Guesses are fine. Blanks are not.
3. **A free account on the form platform.** One email, no card.

**If you are not green-lit at the 24-hour check, I move you to the next room for free and refund you today.** That is not a hurdle. It is the reason the guarantee is honest.

> If you're on the call for the full ninety minutes, you were green-lit, and you leave without a live intake link and a completed test transaction, **I finish it with you one-to-one inside seven days.** If it still isn't done after that, **you don't pay** — reply with the word *refund*, no explanation, no call, no form, done inside 24 hours. **Why fix-first, and say this out loud:** almost nobody who bought a build wants their money back — they want the build. Offering the finish session first converts a refund into a delivered outcome and a testimonial. The refund still exists, unconditionally, at the end of it. **Nothing has been taken from the buyer; a step has been added in their favour.**

## 3. UNIQUE MECHANISM ✓ *(4/5 — kept, two fixes applied)*

The Line is genuinely ownable: a **boundary plus an order of operations**, in a category where everyone else sells a tool list. It survives contact, it's memorable, it's the thumbnail, it's the email, and it's why the offer isn't commodity. Two fixes before shipping.

**Fix 1 — don't over-defend the Gates.** Intake → quote → deposit → delivery is what every Dubsado consultant already sells. **The proprietary asset is the LINE, not the GATES.** The Gates just make it buildable.

**Fix 2 — LIVE AMMUNITION. The Desk side currently includes "listing copy" and "client updates."** Listing copy is *writing in the artist's voice*, and voice is studio-side by any standard this audience applies. The one absolutist claim the business rests on has a hole a hostile commenter finds in about nine seconds, on camera, in front of the exact audience the mechanism exists to disarm.

> **Move "listing copy" off the Desk entirely.**
> **Change "client updates" → "client update *triggers*"** — the system tells you a client is waiting; **you write the message.**

**The Line only works if it is absolute.** An absolute claim with one exception is not a boundary, it is a negotiation — and this audience will negotiate it all the way back to *"so it does write for you."*

### THE STUDIO LINE — final

**Studio Side — 100% human, permanently.** Concept · composition · taste · colour · hands · judgment · the client relationship · **the price** · **anything written in your voice.** There is no image model in the system anywhere, and it *structurally cannot* cross.

**Desk Side — 100% systemized.** Intake · scope capture · quoting · deposits · scheduling · **client update triggers** · revision tracking · delivery · licensing terms · follow-up.

## 9. LEAD MAGNET ✓ *(4/5 — kept, four fixes applied)*

**The strategic call stands and is defensible against anyone:** a buyer whose dominant emotion is *you are trying to take something from me* will not accept a solution before they accept a problem. **The audit must precede the tool, and the tool must precede the price.** Ungating the number is correct. Using the literal on-screen G1 artifact as the payload is correct — it makes the video and the magnet the same object, which almost no funnel achieves.

**Four execution fixes:**

1. **The audit is quietly the largest engineering build in the launch** — conditional logic, scored output, a math toggle, segmentation routing, post-result capture — and no platform or fallback was ever named, which makes it the thing that slips and takes the launch date with it. **Ship it as a hosted form + a scored results page and nothing fancier. Hard gate: if it isn't working by Day 9, ship a locked public spreadsheet with visible formula cells** — ugly and honest beats late, and to *this* buyer the maths being literally in the cells is more trustworthy, not less.
2. **Every payload is a LINK, never an attachment.** V1 sent attachments on the single most important send in the system — a deliverability decision made by accident.
3. **Add an activation event.** Nobody was ever asked whether they actually deployed the free form, **even though deploying it is the strongest purchase predictor available** and the only proof a non-technical buyer gets that they can do this. → Email 3.
4. **Add a share mechanic.** The ungated number means ~45% of completers take the emotional hit and vanish. V1 called that "free distribution" without ever building the thing that would make it true. → *"Copy your number"* on the results page.

## 10. THE 12 QUESTIONS + SCORING MATH ✎ *(was 2/5 — seven arithmetic defects)*

**Why it failed — and this was the most dangerous failure in the document, because the entire trust strategy is "show the math" and the v1 math, shown, indicted itself:**

| # | Defect |
|---|---|
| **a** | **L1 was a category error.** `Q × c × V × f₁` multiplies inquiries by the *close rate* — which isolates the jobs you **won** — then charges a slice of them as lost. It computed a fraction of earned revenue and labeled it a leak. |
| **b** | **L1 and L2 double-counted the same dead job.** An inquiry that went cold from a slow reply *and* a slow quote was billed twice, with no dedupe. |
| **c** | **L3 charged 50% of full job value to anyone who "paid late"** — conflating paid-late (a cash-flow event costing near zero) with never-paid (a total loss) inside one question. |

That left one paid rung against a build comprising Case Zero, a flagship video, a screen-recording plan, a scored audit engine, a Kit, a landing page, seven emails, twenty live builds and fourteen days of support threads — **repeating monthly.** A full-time job paying roughly $2,670 a cycle, with the guarantee handing over the Kit — the largest margin lever in the business — inside a no-questions refund window.

**This is R4 exactly as the researcher wrote it: if it fills, Jean has bought himself a job, and nothing compounds.** The v1 answer was *"stop worrying about its margin"* — a sentence a strategist can afford and an operator cannot.

> **COUNTER-MOVE, three parts.**
> **(a)** **Invert the ladder privately.** The $2,400 Install is sold *first*, by hand, two per month. It is the cash engine that pays for the proof factory — **the room becomes funded work, not speculative work.**
> **(b)** **Cut phase-one build cost hard.** The audit ships as a hosted form with the arithmetic in a spreadsheet. The flagship shrinks to 10:05. Clips ship before it.
> **(c)** **Put a stop-loss on the room, written down before run one:** *if run one produces fewer than eight dated Ledger receipts, the Build Room is a proof factory that does not make proof — stop running it and sell Installs.* **A proof factory that produces no proof is just a job with a live-stream attached**, and the only thing worse than not running it is running it four more times to find that out.

## 16. THE LAUNCH SEQUENCE

**The Conversion Builder's day-numbered execution, running inside the phase gates. ~8 weeks. The order is the point: proof before price, pre-sale before production.**

### PHASE 0 — Prove the buyer exists *(Days 1–14)*

1. **Day 1.** Open YouTube Studio. Write down the **median views of the last 10 uploads.** *The single most important number in the launch — every seat projection is a multiple of it.*
2. **Day 1.** Count the email list. Actual deliverable addresses, not signups.
3. **Day 1.** **Write out by name every artist you personally know who takes 4+ commission inquiries a month.** Not "about sixty." **Names.** If the list is 15, you now know you're 4 seats short *and you have seven weeks to fix it* instead of finding out on launch week.
4. **Day 1–3.** **Thirty cold DMs** to artists with visible commission volume. One question: *"What eats the most unpaid hours in your week?"* No pitch.
5. **Day 14. GATE.** 8+ admin answers, 3+ who'd pay three figures → continue. Answers about finding clients → **stop and rebuild the offer.**

### PHASE 1 — Sell two Installs by hand *(Days 15–35)*

6. **Day 15.** Set up your own payment processor if you don't have one, and charge your own card $1 to confirm a notification lands on screen with a visible amount. *You cannot sell a test transaction you haven't run.*
7. **Day 15–17.** **Build the Studio Line Kit** — intake form, quote logic, deposit gate, and the written language. **Build the product before the marketing for it.**
8. **Day 18–33.** Sell and deliver **two Installs at $2,400** from the Phase 0 conversations. Build their desks by hand. Log everything.
9. **Day 35. GATE.** At least one client consents to be named with a dated deposit receipt → continue. Zero sales after thirty conversations → **reprice the ladder before touching the video.**

### PHASE 2 — Build the proof assets *(Days 36–42)*

10. **Day 36.** Screenshot **every panel of every tool** you'll show on camera. Check for prompt boxes, model pickers, generated images, open browser tabs. *Now, not on shoot day.*
11. **Day 37.** **Record the Negative Proof clip.** One continuous 60s take. **Publish it standalone this week** — it starts the trust work before the video exists. **GATE: this ships before any sales page.**
12. **Day 37.** Write the **Data Boundary Map.** One page.
13. **Day 38–40.** Build the Leak Audit — v2 questions, v2 math, ungated number, arithmetic toggle open by default, $9,999 ceiling, four bands, segmentation routing.
14. **Day 40. HARD GATE.** Not working? **Ship the spreadsheet fallback and move on. Do not move the video date to wait for the audit.**
15. **Day 40.** Build the results page: their number in their own words, four-gate split, **"Copy your number,"** the email ask, the Ledger opt-in.
16. **Day 41.** **Run the audit on the Install client's real numbers.** That output is the figure that goes in the thumbnail, the hook and every email. *It must be real.*
17. **Day 41.** Recruit **three pilot artists.** Free build for footage. **Process claims only.** Get their agreement to share **the audit link only, never the offer** — in writing, with a date.
18. **Day 42.** Write and build the checkout page (§13). **Test a real $150 purchase on your own card and refund it. Time the whole flow on a phone.**

### PHASE 3 — Pre-sell the room *(Days 43–49)*

19. **Day 43.** **Fix the room date, time and timezone.** Put it in the calendar. Nothing downstream starts until the date exists.
20. **Day 43.** Approach **one** guild, Discord or FB group admin. Permission to share the **free audit**, not the offer. Get a yes or no before Day 45 so you know whether those seats exist.
21. **Day 44–48.** **PRE-SELL.** Email the list and DM your named artists: date, promise, guarantee, price. **Collect real cards, not interest.**
22. **Day 49. GATE. 12 paid seats before you film anything.** *If you cannot sell 6 seats to people who already know you, stop and fix the offer before spending a week filming.* **Enthusiasm without cards is a no.**

### PHASE 4 — Produce and publish *(Days 50–56)*

23. **Day 50.** Script lock. Hook (22s), retention map (10:05), four capture points, CTA at 9:40.
24. **Day 51.** Shoot talking-head segments. *First frame is your face, first word is "Nothing."*
25. **Day 52.** Shoot B5 (Gate 1, built in full) and B6 (**the withhold** — quote engine shown running, logic panel never opened).
26. **Day 53.** Shoot **B7 — the 90-second run.** Single unbroken take, on-screen timer, real payment, real amount. Then B8: hard cut to the blank canvas.
27. **Day 54–55.** Edit. Then **watch the full cut on a phone with the sound off, twice, hunting for a prompt box in a single frame** — including a background tab or a reflection. *One frame kills this video.*
28. **Day 55.** Build the thumbnail: `-$[LEAK]` hero left, blank canvas and brush right, hard white line centre. Build the A/B alternate.
29. **Day 55.** Audit link as the **first line** of the description with a plain-English label. Write the pinned comment.
30. **Day 56. PUBLISH.** **Pin the comment within sixty seconds of upload.** Then email your list — send them **the audit, not the video, and never the pitch.**
31. **Day 56.** Turn on the sequence: E1 on capture · E2 +1d · E3 +2d · E4 +3d · E5 +5d · E6 +7d · **E7 six hours before the room.**
32. **Day 57–58.** Cut and publish three clips: the 90-second run, the Negative Proof, the Line drawn by hand. **Audit link on every one.** Gain thumbnail on clips only.
33. **Day 58.** Pilot artists share the audit. Group admin posts it. *Agreed in writing on Days 41 and 43 — this is a reminder, not a request.*
34. **Day 59–61.** **DM every name on your Day 1 list. Personal messages, one at a time, sending the audit.** No broadcast. **This is the highest-yield work in the entire launch and it is also the work you will most want to skip.**
35. **Day 62.** Count paid seats. **Under 12 → do not add paid traffic.** Extend by one week and keep DMing. *A thin room with real receipts beats a full room you can't repeat.*

### PHASE 5 — Run it *(Days 63–64)*

36. **Day 63.** Reminders at 24h **with the pre-work**, then 1h, then 10 minutes. The 1h reminder carries a proactive transfer offer: *"Life happened? Reply 'next' and take the following date."* **Every transfer is a saved refund.**
37. **Day 64. RUN THE ROOM.** Cameras off, announced at minute zero. **Anonymous volume poll at minute two, no names.** Build G1, G2, G3. **Minute 84: everyone pastes their live URL into one link** — guarantee verification, readable in ten seconds. **Minute 88: book a 15-minute follow-up with anyone not on that list.** **Minute 84–90 is the ONLY mention of the Full Line**, delivered after their money has landed, once, then stop.
38. **Day 64, same evening.** Send the Ledger ask: amount and date, nothing else. **Refund anyone who asks, inside 24 hours, without a single question.** *The first person who screenshots a fast no-questions refund is worth more than the $150.*
39. **Day 65.** Replace every `[ASSUMED]` with what you measured — view→click, audit completion, and above all **the qualified percentage.** If it comes back at 30% instead of 65%, the model isn't broken; you need twice the traffic, **and now you know it.**
40. **Day 65. THE STOP-LOSS.** Fewer than 8 dated Ledger receipts → **stop running the room and sell Installs.**

**The three things that would make this launch fail, in order of likelihood:** the audit slipping and taking the video date with it *(step 14 exists to stop that)* · discovering late that the DM list was never 60 people *(step 3 exists to stop that)* · running a room against a guarantee you don't have time per person to clear *(step 37 and the cap at 20 exist to stop that)*.
