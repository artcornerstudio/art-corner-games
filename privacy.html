🔒 Privacy Policy

Plain-language version — last updated September 2026.

The short version

Spread Out! collects nothing. There's no sign-up, no account, no ads, and nothing about your child is ever sent anywhere. Everything the game remembers (player names, jerseys, stars, best scores) is saved only in your own browser, on your own device — the same way a browser remembers a website you visited before.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't have a server that stores information about players. We don't use analytics, ad networks, or tracking pixels. There is no way for us to see who is playing, how often, or how well.

What's saved on your device

The game uses your browser's built-in storage (called "localStorage") to remember, only on the device you're playing on:

  • Player names you type in, jersey colors and numbers
  • Stars earned, best scores, and match wins
  • Whether the sound is muted

This information never leaves your device. It isn't sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser's site data, or using a private/ incognito window, will erase it.

Accounts and sign-in

There are none. Player profiles are just names you type in — not real accounts, no passwords, no email addresses, nothing verified.

Ads and third parties

No ads, ever. The game makes no calls to any outside service while you play — no analytics, no font downloads, no images from other websites. Once the page has loaded, it can even work with no internet connection at all.

Kids and COPPA

Spread Out! is designed for children roughly ages 6–12. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, of any age, there is nothing for us to disclose, sell, or misuse. We built it this way on purpose.

Changes to this page

If this policy ever changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Since the game collects nothing today, any future change that added data collection would be a big change — and we'd make that very clear.
