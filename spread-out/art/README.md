# Spread Out! art

The night stadium behind the menu was made in October 2026 with OpenArt
(Nano Banana 2.1, text to image) and is saved in the Art Corner OpenArt library.

* `stadium-tall.webp` (1080 wide) is used on phones and other tall screens.
* `stadium-wide.webp` (1920 wide) is used on computers and tablets held sideways.

To bring in a new picture from OpenArt, add a line to `art/fetch.txt` at the top
of the repository and push. The "Fetch art" GitHub workflow saves it here as WebP.
