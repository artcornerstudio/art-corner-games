# Spread Out! sound effects

Real recordings, all **Creative Commons 0** (public domain: free to use in a paid
game, no credit required). Each was downloaded from Freesound, then trimmed,
levelled and saved as a small mono MP3. The game loads them after the first tap and
falls back to its built-in synthesized sound for any clip that has not loaded.

| File | Used for | Source (Freesound, CC0) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `kick.mp3` | every kick and pass, and shots (played lower) | [Soccer Kick.wav by musita182](https://freesound.org/s/261267/) |
| `whistle.mp3` | kick-off (one blast), full time (three) | [Referee whistle blow, gymnasium.wav by SpliceSound](https://freesound.org/s/218318/) |
| `crowd.mp3` | the stadium crowd under Mini Match (a seamless loop) | [Stadium Crowd by stomachache](https://freesound.org/s/274516/) |
| `goal.mp3` | the roar when your team scores | [Crowd cheer.wav by BeeProductive](https://freesound.org/s/430046/) |
| `aww.mp3` | when the other team scores or a shot is blocked | [crowd `oh - - disappointed.mp3 by mrrap4food](https://freesound.org/s/619007/) |

`crowd.mp3` carries 0.1 s of wrapped-around audio at each end; the game loops
between 0.1 s and (length - 0.1 s), so the loop has no gap.

The ball bounce, the star sparkle, the fanfare and the music are made by the
game itself in the browser (no files).
