# ArtCorner Communication Templates (CT-01 … CT-16)

All templates are PREPARE-tier drafts until Jean approves them as standards; CT-01 is the candidate for the first EXECUTE-within-policy grant. Voice rules: `docs/03-brand-voice-canon-guide.md`. Bracketed fields are merge fields; **a template may never be sent with an unfilled bracket or an `[AWAITING JEAN: …]` placeholder.**

---

### CT-01 · Inquiry acknowledgment (all lanes)
> Hi [name] — thanks so much for reaching out to ArtCorner. Jean reviews every project personally, and we'd love to learn more about what you have in mind. [One lane-specific question set follows — see CT-02/04/08.] We'll get back to you with next steps as soon as we hear from you. — The ArtCorner Studio, Reading, PA

### CT-02 · Mural intake follow-up
> To put a real concept and quote together, Jean needs a few specifics about the wall: (1) width and height, (2) surface — brick, drywall, concrete, block — and its condition, (3) interior or exterior, (4) two or three photos of the wall and the room/site, (5) anything already planned for the space. Once we have these, we'll propose the next step (often a quick site visit).

### CT-03 · Quote cover note
> Here's the proposal for [project]. It covers exactly what's included, what isn't, the timeline as proposed, and terms. Two things worth knowing about how we work: the final artistic direction always comes from Jean, and any change in scope gets a fresh quote so there are never surprises. Questions welcome — happy to walk through it.

### CT-04 · Class enrollment offer
> Good news — [class name] has a spot for [student first name]. It meets [schedule], and here's what the course covers: [2–3 lines from approved curriculum]. Tuition is [rate from matrix] . To enroll, just complete the attached form — it only asks for what we genuinely need. Questions about fit or level? Ask away.

### CT-05 · Photo policy notice (youth programs & events)
> A quick note on photos: we only ever photograph students or guests with separate written permission, and we'll always tell you before any photo is shared anywhere. No permission form, no photos — simple as that.

### CT-06 · Class welcome packet
> Welcome to ArtCorner! Here's everything for the first session: **Where:** the studio in Antietam, PA [address on confirmation]. **Bring:** [materials list]; we provide [provided list]. **Expect:** small groups, original projects, and steady encouragement — talent deserves support. If anything comes up before class, reply here.

### CT-07 · Consent form cover (photo/testimonial/artwork feature)
> We'd be honored to share [work/photo] — and we never do that without your written OK. The attached form says exactly what would be shared, where, and that you can withdraw permission anytime. Take your time with it; nothing happens until it's signed.

### CT-08 · Event space & setup note (to host)
> To make [event] run smoothly, here's what the space needs: [table/space list], access [time] before start, and [water/floor-protection notes]. We bring everything else. For groups with kids, we ask that [supervision expectation] — it keeps the fun safe.

### CT-09 · Event thank-you & rebooking
> Thank you for having ArtCorner at [event] — [one genuine specific detail]. It was a pleasure. If you'd ever like to do it again (birthdays have a way of coming back around), you know where to find us.

### CT-10 · Concept review invitation (mural/commission)
> Jean has the concept ready for your review: [delivery method]. Look at it with fresh eyes and tell us honestly what lands and what doesn't — specific reactions ("the left side feels crowded") help more than polite ones. We log every note verbatim so nothing gets lost.

### CT-11 · Project progress update
> Quick update on [project]: [milestone reached]. Next: [next step] around [proposed window]. Nothing is needed from you right now — this is just so you're never wondering. / — this one needs your input: [specific ask].

### CT-12 · Mural care instructions
> Your mural is done — here's how to keep it looking new: [surface-specific care from the production record: cleaning, direct-sun notes, touch-up contact]. If anything ever happens to the wall, contact us before any repair — most things are fixable if we see them early.

### CT-13 · Warm decline — licensed/copyrighted characters
> We love that [character/franchise] is a favorite! We can't paint or teach copyrighted characters we don't have permission for — that protects you as much as us. What we *can* do is design an original [hero/princess/creature] made just for [name/space] — one nobody else in the world has. Want to see what Jean would come up with?

### CT-14 · Proof feedback request
> Here's the current proof for [commission]. This round is for your honest notes — what to keep, what to adjust. Your agreement includes [N] revision rounds, and we'll tell you clearly if a request goes beyond the original scope before anything changes.

### CT-15 · Partnership pass (gracious decline)
> Thank you for thinking of ArtCorner — [genuine specific about their work]. Right now we can't take this on and do it justice, and we'd rather be honest than half-committed. We'd be glad to stay in touch for the future.

### CT-16 · Issue acknowledgment (first response)
> [Name], thank you for telling us — I'm sorry this wasn't the experience it should have been. We're looking into exactly what happened right now, and you'll hear back from us by [specific time] with what we found and how we'll make it right.
