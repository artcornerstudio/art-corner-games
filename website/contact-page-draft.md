Status: **DRAFT — awaiting Jean** (gate: external publish). Prepared by Content & Communications (14); reviewed against the Brand Guide (`docs/03`) and Truth Ledger (`docs/01`).
Fixes audit item #1 in `docs/09-website-audit-2026-10-16.md`: the homepage had no contact link, email, or contact page.

Rules this draft follows: no prices (pricing matrix is empty), no response-time promises, no street address or phone (not on record), no data collected about children beyond what's needed, only Truth-Ledger services.

## 1. Page setup in Hostinger Website Builder

1. **Pages → Add page → Blank** — name it `Contact`, URL `/contact`.
2. Add a **"Contact / Book" button** to the site header (navigation) linking to `/contact`. Also add one at the bottom of the homepage.
3. Add the sections below in order. Use your existing theme's fonts and colors.
4. Add a **Contact form** element; in its settings, set **"Send submissions to": `jeanesther@artcornerstudio.net`**.
5. Make the email address a link: select the text → link → **Email** → `jeanesther@artcornerstudio.net`.
6. Publish, then send a test inquiry to yourself. It should appear in the Hostinger mailbox and in Gmail (via the forwarder).

## 2. Page copy (paste as-is, edit freely)

### Heading (the page's only H1)
**Let's make something together**

### Intro paragraph
Whether you're picturing a mural for a blank wall, a portrait of someone you love, or a class for a curious young artist, it starts with a message. Jean Esther reads every inquiry personally and will reply with next steps.

### Direct contact line
Prefer email? Write to **jeanesther@artcornerstudio.net**
Studio in Antietam, PA, serving Reading and Berks County.

### Three ways we work (three columns or cards)

**Create and Learn**
Private lessons, senior classes, art parties, workshops, and caricatures at your event.

**Transform a Place**
Custom murals, public art, microcement walls, and projection mapping for homes, businesses, and communities.

**Build an Artist Life**
Consulting and artist development for emerging and established artists, rooted in *How to Be a Successful Artist*.

### What happens next (numbered — it's a real sequence)
1. **We read your message.** Every inquiry goes to Jean.
2. **We ask what we need to know.** For a mural that's usually wall size, surface, and a few photos; for a class, the student's age group and experience.
3. **Jean proposes a plan.** You'll get a clear scope and quote before anything is scheduled.

### Section heading above the form
**Tell us about your project**

### Small print under the form
We use your details only to reply to this inquiry and never share them. If your request involves children, please have a parent, guardian, or school staff member send it.

### Message shown after someone submits
Thank you — your message is on its way to Jean. Watch your inbox (and spam folder) for a reply.
