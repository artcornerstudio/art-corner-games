# WF-01 · Mural Inquiry → Delivered Mural

Owner: Murals & Public Art Pod (15) · Priority default: P2 · Status: READY FOR APPROVAL

**Trigger:** inquiry mentioning mural, wall art, public art, microcement, or projection install (web form, email, referral, RFP found by Research).

## Steps

1. **Intake** (Orchestrator → Customer Relations). Log `MUR-<id>`. Checklist: contact info; property type & ownership/authority to commission; wall location (interior/exterior); **dimensions**; **surface material & condition**; photos; desired theme/goals; timeline hopes; budget conversation readiness; youth/community participation intended?
   - *Decision:* checklist incomplete → send follow-up question set (template CT-02). **Never proceed to concept/quote on missing dimensions or surface data — HOLD.**
2. **Qualification** (Research): fit score; municipal/RFP items flagged for Jean early.
3. **Technical packet** (Murals Pod): verify feasibility notes (access, lift, weather window, prep needs incl. microcement). Unknowns → site-visit proposal instead of guesses.
4. **Site visit** (Jean): pod proposes windows from capacity ledger; Jean confirms. Visit notes return to the packet.
5. **Concept brief → Jean** (gate: final artistic direction). Jean produces/approves concept. Client concept review uses template CT-10; client feedback logged verbatim.
6. **Quote** (Proposals & Quotes): from matrix + cost pass-throughs; Finance conformity + capacity verdict; Brand + QA certification → Jean PREPARE queue (gate: pricing, contract).
7. **Approval & contract** (Jean): contract terms Jean-gated; permits/municipal permissions flagged — Jean or client files them.
8. **Production plan** (pod): schedule, materials, prep, safety, contingency. Youth participation → Safety safeguard checklist required.
9. **Execution** (Jean paints; pod tracks milestones, prepares client updates via CT-11).
10. **Closeout:** final walkthrough note; care instructions (CT-12); photo/portfolio consent (gate: client photos → Jean); assets archived; Customer Experience follow-up at +2 weeks; record to Analytics.

## Fail-safe behaviors (tested in `tests/adversarial-evaluation.md` A1)
- Missing dimensions → HOLD + specific request, never estimate.
- Copyrighted characters requested in design → Safety FAIL → warm decline + original alternative (CT-13).
- Rate missing from matrix → quote HOLDs with `[AWAITING JEAN: rate]`, surfaces in digest §1.
