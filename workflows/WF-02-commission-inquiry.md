# WF-02 · Commission Inquiry → Delivered Piece

Owner: Commissions & Originals Pod (16) · Priority default: P2 · Status: READY FOR APPROVAL

**Trigger:** inquiry for portrait, fine art, logo, pinstriping, foam sculpture, or digital painting.

## Steps
1. **Intake** (`COM-<id>`): contact; commission type; subject; references supplied (rights confirmed by client — portraits from photos need the client's statement they may use the photo; **subject is a minor → Safety flag**); size/medium; intended use (esp. logos: where used, exclusivity?); timeline hopes.
2. **Brief** (pod): written brief; ambiguities resolved by one consolidated question set, not assumptions.
3. **Scope doc** (pod): inclusions/exclusions; revision rounds per Jean's standard terms; change-order policy stated.
4. **Quote** (Proposals): matrix rates only → Finance → Brand → QA → **Jean gate (pricing/terms)**. Missing rate → HOLD placeholder.
5. **Acceptance:** deposit terms per matrix; recorded by Finance on Jean's confirmation.
6. **Proof ladder:** Jean sketch/proof → client feedback (logged verbatim, CT-14) → Jean revises within scope. Out-of-scope asks → change order → reprice (Finance) → Jean.
7. **Final approval** (client sign-off) → **Jean gate: release of final art** → delivery prep (packaging/shipping or handoff notes).
8. **Closeout:** delivery confirmation; care/usage notes (logos: usage rights doc per Jean's terms); portfolio consent (gate) ; Customer Experience follow-up; Analytics record.

## Fail-safes
- Disney/licensed-character commission request → Safety FAIL → CT-13 warm decline + original-design alternative (tested, A2).
- Scope creep mid-proof → change order, never silent absorption.
- Client pressure for a date → capacity verdict first; dates only from Jean-confirmed schedule.
