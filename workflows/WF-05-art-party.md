# WF-05 · Art Party / Workshop / Event Booking

Owner: Events & Exhibitions Pod (18) · Priority default: P2 · Status: READY FOR APPROVAL

**Trigger:** party, workshop, community event, or event-caricature inquiry.

## Steps
1. **Intake** (`EVT-<id>`): occasion & date hopes; headcount & ages (**minors → Safety checklist**); venue (client's, studio, other); duration; activity preference; accessibility needs; caricature events: expected throughput.
2. **Capacity & date:** Finance capacity verdict; date windows proposed from Jean's stated availability; **venue/date confirmation is a Jean gate**.
3. **Event scope** (pod): activity plan, supplies with quantities, space/setup needs sent to host (CT-08).
4. **Quote** (Proposals): matrix (missing rate → HOLD) → Finance → Brand → QA → **Jean gate**; deposit terms per matrix.
5. **Registration** (if open-enrollment workshop): approved minimal-data form; cap and waitlist tracked.
6. **Run-of-show** (pod): timeline, Jean's schedule, helpers, contingency; minors → photo policy announced in invite/entry materials.
7. **Execution:** day-of checklist; pod prepares, Jean delivers.
8. **Closeout:** supplies reconciliation; photos handled per consent (publishing → Jean gate); thank-you + rebooking (CT-09; birthdays are natural annual rebooks); CSAT ask; Analytics record.

## Fail-safes
- Requested theme uses licensed characters → CT-13 warm decline + original theme alternative.
- Headcount exceeding safe supervision for ages → HOLD with revised format options.
- Date not confirmable (Jean availability unknown) → holds proposed, digest asks Jean.
