# WF-06 · Book / Product Concept → Launch-Ready

Owner: Publishing & Products Pod (19) · Priority default: P3 · Status: READY FOR APPROVAL

**Trigger:** Jean proposes a title/product; or Offering Architect promotes a candidate (e.g., PROPOSED hometown-themed books) to pilot with Jean's approval.

## Steps
1. **Concept record** (`PUB-<id>`): title concept, audience, format, pathway fit (Read/Give), canon involvement (Meta Sabian? → Librarian co-review from day one).
2. **Feasibility** (pod + Finance): production requirements, printer spec options, cost inputs **as researched quotes presented to Jean — no invented costs**; capacity impact on Jean's hours.
3. **Gate — Jean: proceed to production?** (If from Offering Architect: pilot classification required first.)
4. **Content production:** Jean creates; pod structures, prepares layouts, proofreads (mechanical errors only — never rewrites Jean's voice or method); youth titles → full youth-content checklist + Safety.
5. **Canon check** (Meta Sabian titles): five canonical elements only; anything new is Jean's addition via Truth Ledger change control **before** it appears in print.
6. **Production files:** print/digital files to spec → QA certification against Jean-approved proof → **Jean gate: final proof approval**.
7. **Launch package:** description, cover assets, launch content plan (via Content & Communications), listing package (via E-commerce) — all factual, no invented reviews/claims.
8. **Gate — Jean: publish/print order** (spending + external commitment). Printer contracts Jean-signed.
9. **Closeout:** assets archived; product record opened for S6; Analytics baseline for the title starts at zero real data.

## Fail-safes
- Lore gap discovered mid-layout → "not established" → HOLD to Jean (tested, A5).
- Printer spec conflict with approved proof → QA rejects files, never silently adjusts art.
- Launch copy claiming sales/reviews that don't exist → Brand strike (UNKNOWN fence).
