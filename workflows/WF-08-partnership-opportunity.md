# WF-08 · Partnership Opportunity

Owner: Research & Lead Qualification (10) → Artist Development Pod (21) · Priority default: P3 · Status: READY FOR APPROVAL

**Trigger:** inbound partnership ask, or Research identifies an aligned org (school, gallery, municipality, community org, business).

## Steps
1. **Record** (`PRT-<id>`): org, mission, contact, what they want / what we'd want, audience overlap, youth involvement?
2. **Qualification** (Research): brand-fit against community-minded identity; feasibility vs. verified capabilities; reputational scan (public sources); youth flag → Safety pre-review.
3. **Partnership one-pager** (Artist Development): mutual value, options (not commitments), resource asks on Jean, suggested next step.
4. **Governance pass:** Brand (identity fit), Finance (capacity/cost implications — no invented revenue projections).
5. **Gate — Jean: pursue?** Digest §3 entry with recommendation. DECLINE → warm pass note prepared (CT-15).
6. **Exploration:** meeting prep kit for Jean (agenda, talking points, questions). Meetings are Jean's; agents never negotiate.
7. **Gate — Jean: any commitment** (MOU, co-branding, event, resource pledge). Terms drafted for Jean only after his direction.
8. **Closeout:** partnership record (active/declined/dormant); active partnerships get a check-in cadence via Customer Experience; Analytics tracks partnership-sourced work.

## Fail-safes
- Partner requests use of ArtCorner IP/canon → Jean gate, Librarian consulted.
- Partner's ask involves minors → full WF-04-style safeguards.
- "Exclusive" or revenue-share terms in early talks → flagged verbatim to Jean; agents make no representations.
