# WF-12 · New Offer Pilot

Owner: Opportunity & Offering Architect (23) · Priority default: P3 · Status: READY FOR APPROVAL

**Trigger:** new idea from Jean, a customer pattern, a Research finding, or a PROPOSED Truth-Ledger item (e.g., ArtCorner Arcade, hometown books, AI-assisted children's video).

## Stages (idea classification lifecycle)
`RESEARCH → CANDIDATE → PILOT → APPROVED → (or PAUSED at any point)` — stage changes are logged; **only Jean moves anything to PILOT or APPROVED; nothing ever auto-joins the live menu.**

## Steps
1. **Idea record** (`IDEA-<id>`): source, one-paragraph concept, pathway/audience hypothesis.
2. **RESEARCH:** Architect evaluates — brand fit (Brand consult), customer need evidence (real signals only), capacity cost on Jean, expected costs/margins (**inputs researched or Jean-supplied; no invented economics**), safety/rights surface (Safety consult — mandatory for anything youth-facing or AI-assisted).
3. **CANDIDATE brief:** one page — the offer, evidence, risks, pilot design (small, reversible, measurable), kill criteria.
4. **Gate — Jean: run pilot?** (≤1 such decision per digest, ranked.)
5. **PILOT:** delivered through the owning pod with explicit pilot labeling; safety rules apply at full strength (pilots get zero safety slack); metrics collected per pilot design.
6. **Readout:** results vs. kill criteria, honest including "insufficient data."
7. **Gate — Jean: APPROVED (→ catalog + Truth Ledger update via Librarian), iterate, or PAUSED.** Catalog/pricing additions follow their own gates.

## Fail-safes
- AI-assisted children's content: cannot pass CANDIDATE without a rights-verification plan and Safety's written standard — currently PROPOSED and held there.
- Pilot leaking into public marketing as a "new service" → Brand FAIL (not on live menu).
- Sunk-cost pressure ("we built it, ship it") → kill criteria decide, Jean rules.
